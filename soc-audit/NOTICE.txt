--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`frontend/public/logos/` contains logo artwork for the following vendors:
Cisco, Dell, Fortinet, HPE, Huawei, Juniper, MikroTik, Palo Alto Networks,
Sophos, TP-Link, Ubiquiti and WatchGuard.

Each of those names and logos is a trademark or registered trademark of its
respective owner. They are used here **nominatively**, solely to identify the
equipment vendor in the network-inventory screens, and are NOT an endorsement
by, affiliation with, or sponsorship from any of those companies.

Before redistributing this repository you must satisfy each vendor's brand
guidelines. Most of them permit nominative use of an unmodified logo at a
reasonable size; some (notably Cisco, Fortinet and Palo Alto Networks) require
written permission or restrict recolouring and resizing. If you cannot satisfy
a given vendor's terms, remove that vendor's logo from `frontend/public/logos/`
and drop the corresponding entry from the `VENDOR_LOGOS` map in
`frontend/src/pages/NetworkDevicesPage.jsx` — the page falls back to a generic
icon for vendors without artwork.
